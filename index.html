Questions

Before you send a request.

Do you film in the US?

No. We work remotely. You film on your phone or camera, and we coach your shots and handle the edit. That keeps costs far below hiring a crew.

How fast will I get my video?

Short-form edits take 2–3 business days. Long-form edits take 4–7 business days. Seoul is 13–16 hours ahead of the US, so we often work while you sleep.

How is long-form priced?

Per minute of the finished video, from up to 60 minutes of raw footage. A 10-minute Premium video is 10 × $60 = $600.

What if I don't like the edit?

Leave comments on the draft and we revise. We keep going until you're happy with it.

How do I pay?

We confirm the scope and price with you by email first. Then we send a secure Stripe payment link so you can pay by card in USD. You never pay before you approve the quote.

Will language be a problem?

No. We work in English by email and on video calls, and all captions and on-screen text are in English.
